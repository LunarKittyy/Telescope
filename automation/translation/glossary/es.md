# Spanish (neutral Latin American, understood in Spain too) glossary and style

The agreed terms for this language, built from the Swedish glossary's term list. Use them in every text. If one seems wrong, keep it and raise an objection with the reason. Never change a term silently.

Avoid: remover, salvar, soportar, librería, customizar, resetear, accesar, performancia, cliquear, ordenador, fichero

## Style
- Use informal second-person singular ("tú" / tuteo neutro, e.g. "conecta tu teléfono", "intenta de nuevo"). It is the standard used across modern mobile and desktop apps (Google/Android, Apple, Microsoft) in Latin America and is fully understood and natural in Spain. Never use formal "usted" (too stiff) or regional "vos".
- Plain, modern, everyday Spanish (español neutro cotidiano). No stiff bureaucratic phrasing, no false friends, no slang. Use sentence case.
- Buttons and action triggers use infinitives ("Agregar teléfono", "Reintentar", "Actualizar y reiniciar"). Statuses are active gerunds ("Transmitiendo por Wi-Fi") or short states ("Listo").
- Keep the English's brevity. A note saying "keep short" is a hard limit.
- Keep the English " - " (spaced hyphen). No en or em dashes. Use standard straight double quotation marks ("...") matching the English.
- In prose, numbers take a decimal point (2.4 GHz) and a space before % and °C ("{level} %", "%2$d %%").
- Questions in dialogs: "¿Quieres…?" (e.g. "¿Quieres reiniciar ahora?").
- Card names in prose: "la tarjeta X" (la tarjeta Cámara web).
- Pronouns and gender: Telescope on desktop is "el programa" or "Telescope" (masculine). The phone app is "la app" or "la aplicación" (feminine).
- Keep untranslated: Telescope, OBS, adb, VA-API, PyAV, VB-Cable, VB-Audio, v4l2loopback, FFmpeg, USB, Wi-Fi, JPEG, MJPEG, H.264, fps, Nightly, file names, commands, {fields}, %1$s.

## Framing speeds, slowest first (keep short)
Caracol, Lento, Pausado, Calmo, Sereno, Firme, Ágil, Rápido, Veloz, Rayo

## Room to move (keep short)
Off, Fidget, Shift, Lean, Step, Roam -> Desactivado, Juguetear, Acomodarse, Inclinarse, Dar un paso, Caminar. Its tooltip: "juguetear, acomodarte en la silla, inclinarte, dar un paso o caminar"
