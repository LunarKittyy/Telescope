# Swedish glossary and style

The terms agreed when both apps were first translated (October 2026, by three models and the maintainer). Use them in every text. If one seems wrong, keep it and raise an objection with the reason. Never change a term silently.

Avoid: ladda ned, mick, endast, denna, detta, dessa, erhålla, tillhandahålla, hårdvaruaccelerering

## Style
- Use du-form, in plain everyday Swedish (vardaglig sakprosa). No kanslisvenska, no slang. Use sentence case.
- Prefer "bara" over "endast", "den här" over "denna", and short words over long ones.
- Buttons are imperatives ("Lägg till telefon", "Försök igen"). Statuses are finite verbs ("Strömmar via Wi-Fi") or short states ("Redo").
- Keep the English's brevity. A note saying "keep short" is a hard limit.
- Keep the English " - " (spaced hyphen). No en or em dashes. Use the same quotation marks as the English.
- In prose, numbers take a decimal comma (2,4 GHz) and a space before % and °C ("{level} %", "%2$d %%").
- Questions in dialogs: "Vill du …?"
- Card names in prose: "kortet X" (kortet Webbläsarkamera).
- Pronouns: Telescope on the desktop is "det" (programmet). The phone app is "appen", so "den".
- Keep untranslated: Telescope, OBS, adb, VA-API, PyAV, VB-Cable, VB-Audio, v4l2loopback, FFmpeg, USB, Wi-Fi, JPEG, MJPEG, H.264, fps, Nightly, file names, commands, {fields}, %1$s.
