# Vietnamese glossary and style

The agreed terms for Vietnamese. Use them in every text. If one seems wrong, keep it and raise an objection with the reason. Never change a term silently.

Avoid: tải xuống, kích chuột, nhắp chuột, tiến hành, thực hiện, tái kết nối, máy vi tính, điện thoại di động, gỡ bỏ cài đặt, phần cứng tăng tốc

## Style
- Address the user with "bạn" (friendly, natural, standard in modern apps like Google, Android, and Apple). Omit the pronoun when natural, especially on buttons, menus, and short prompts. Avoid stiff pronouns like "quý khách" or hierarchical ones like "anh/chị/em".
- Register: Plain, natural, contemporary Vietnamese (tiếng Việt hiện đại, tự nhiên). Avoid translationese, bureaucratic Sino-Vietnamese phrasing where simpler everyday words exist, and slang. Use sentence case.
- Buttons are imperatives ("Thêm điện thoại", "Thử lại"). Statuses are verbs with aspect ("Đang truyền phát qua Wi-Fi") or short states ("Sẵn sàng").
- Keep the English's brevity. A note saying "keep short" is a hard limit.
- Keep the English " - " (spaced hyphen). No en or em dashes. Use the same quotation marks as the English.
- In prose, numbers take a decimal comma (2,4 GHz) and a space before % and °C ("{level} %", "%2$d %%").
- Questions in dialogs: "Bạn có muốn…?" or natural concise question forms.
- Card names in prose: "thẻ X" (thẻ Camera trình duyệt).
- Pronouns: Refer to Telescope on desktop and phone as "ứng dụng" ("ứng dụng này") or by name ("Telescope").
- Keep untranslated: Telescope, OBS, adb, VA-API, PyAV, VB-Cable, VB-Audio, v4l2loopback, FFmpeg, USB, Wi-Fi, JPEG, MJPEG, H.264, fps, Nightly, file names, commands, {fields}, %1$s.
