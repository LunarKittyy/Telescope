# Italian glossary and style

The agreed terms for Italian. Use them in every text. If one seems wrong, keep it and raise an objection with the reason. Never change a term silently.

Avoid: cancellare (per "cancel": usa "annulla"; per "delete": usa "elimina"), settaggi, settare, stoppare, restartare, downloadare, customizzare, killare, device, effettuare, provvedere, Lei, Voi, scaricamento

## Style
- Address the user with informal "tu" (second-person singular: "Collega il telefono", "Vuoi…?"). Never use formal "Lei" or archaic "voi". This is the standard used by modern apps and platforms in Italian (Android, iOS, macOS, Windows).
- Use natural, everyday Italian (italiano comune, chiaro e amichevole). No burocratese, no stiff corporate phrasing, no unnecessary anglicisms or calques. Use sentence case (maiuscolo solo all'inizio della frase e per i nomi propri).
- Prefer short, direct words over long ones (e.g. "scarica" over "esegui il download", "elimina" over "provvedi all'eliminazione").
- Buttons are imperatives or infinitives ("Aggiungi telefono", "Riprova", "Copia diagnostica"). Statuses are short states or present descriptions ("In streaming via Wi-Fi", "Pronto").
- Keep the English's brevity. A note saying "keep short" is a hard limit.
- Keep the English " - " (spaced hyphen). No en or em dashes. Use the same quotation marks as the English.
- In prose, numbers take a decimal comma (2,4 GHz) and a space before % and °C ("{level} %", "%2$d %%", "35 °C").
- Questions in dialogs: "Vuoi…?"
- Card names in prose: "la scheda X" (la scheda Fotocamera del browser).
- Pronouns: Telescope on the desktop is masculine ("il programma" or "Telescope"). The phone app is "l'app" (feminine).
- Keep untranslated: Telescope, OBS, adb, VA-API, PyAV, VB-Cable, VB-Audio, v4l2loopback, FFmpeg, USB, Wi-Fi, JPEG, MJPEG, H.264, fps, Nightly, file names, commands, {fields}, %1$s.
