# Simplified Chinese glossary and style

The agreed terms for Simplified Chinese. Use them in every text. If one seems wrong, keep it and raise an objection with the reason. Never change a term silently.

Avoid: 您, 该, 进行, 被 (unnatural passive), 软体, 硬体, 荧幕, 影片, 程式, 预设 (for default), 激活 (for enable), 登入, 重新启动

## Style
- Use "你" (informal, direct, friendly) rather than "您" (which feels overly formal, distant, or like customer service). Popular Simplified Chinese apps (WeChat, Bilibili) and platforms (Android, Windows, Apple) use "你". Omit the pronoun altogether when the context is clear (e.g., "选择相机" rather than "选择你的相机").
- Plain, natural, everyday Simplified Chinese (平实自然、简练口语化). Avoid bureaucratic phrasing (公文腔), stiff legalese ("该设备"), and Europeanized translationese (欧化中文 / 翻译腔).
- Avoid empty filler verbs like "进行" ("进行配对" -> "配对") and unnatural passive voice with "被" ("手机已被连接" -> "手机已连接").
- Buttons are short action verbs or imperatives ("添加手机", "重试", "开始", "停止"). Statuses are concise descriptions or states ("正在通过 Wi-Fi 串流", "未连接", "就绪").
- Keep the English's brevity. A note saying "keep short" is a hard limit. Chinese text is naturally compact; keep phrases concise.
- Keep the English " - " (spaced hyphen). No em dashes where a spaced hyphen is used in the original.
- Quotation marks: use full-width curved quotation marks “” and ‘’ in prose. In code, identifiers, or format strings, preserve ASCII quotes.
- In prose, numbers take a decimal point (2.4 GHz). Do NOT put a space before % or °C ("{level}%", "%2$d%%", "35°C").
- Spacing: insert a single half-width space between Chinese characters and English words or numbers (e.g., "通过 Wi-Fi 串流", "USB 调试"), but no space between numbers and % or °C.
- Questions in dialogs: "是否要……？" or "要……吗？"
- Card names in prose: “X”卡片 (e.g. “快捷键”卡片).
- Pronouns: Telescope desktop app is "此应用" or "桌面端". The phone app is "手机应用" or "手机端".
- Keep untranslated: Telescope, OBS, adb, VA-API, PyAV, VB-Cable, VB-Audio, v4l2loopback, FFmpeg, USB, Wi-Fi, JPEG, MJPEG, H.264, fps, Nightly, file names, commands, {fields}, %1$s.

## Framing speeds, slowest first (keep short)
蜗牛, 树懒, 漫步, 从容, 沉稳, 稳健, 轻快, 敏捷, 飞速, 闪电

## Room to move (keep short)
Off, Fidget, Shift, Lean, Step, Roam -> 关, 小动, 挪身, 探身, 迈步, 溜达. Its tooltip: "做小动作、在椅子上挪身、探身、迈步或四处溜达"
