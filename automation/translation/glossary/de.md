# German glossary and style

The agreed terms for German. Use them in every text. If one seems wrong, keep it and raise an objection with the reason. Never change a term silently.

Avoid: downloaden, lediglich, mittels, zwecks, Präferenzen, Applikation, selektieren, editieren, Default, tappen, Instanz

## Style
- Use du-form (lowercase: du, dein, dir), which is the standard across popular modern German apps (Apple, Android, Spotify, Discord). Never use the formal address (capitalised Sie, Ihr, Ihnen) or capitalised "Du".
- Plain everyday German (klare, natürliche Alltagssprache). No officialese (Beamtendeutsch), no stiff jargon, no slang. Use sentence case.
- Prefer "nur" over "lediglich", natural phrasing over stiff demonstratives ("dieser/diese/dieses"), and short words over long compounds.
- Buttons and menu actions use infinitives ("Smartphone hinzufügen", "Erneut versuchen", "Jetzt neu starten"). Statuses are finite verbs ("Streamt über Wi-Fi") or short states ("Bereit").
- Keep the English's brevity. A note saying "keep short" is a hard limit. German tends to expand, so actively condense phrases to fit compact UI spaces.
- Keep the English " - " (spaced hyphen). No en or em dashes. For quotation marks in prose, use German typographical quotes („…“) or straight quotes ("...") matching the English; avoid English curly quotes (“…”).
- In prose and UI, numbers take a decimal comma (2,4 GHz) and a space before % and °C ("{level} %", "%2$d %%", "45 °C").
- Questions in dialogs: "Möchtest du …?"
- Card names in prose: "die Karte X" (die Karte Browser-Kamera).
- Pronouns: Telescope on the desktop is "es" (das Programm). The phone app is "sie" (die App).
- Keep untranslated: Telescope, OBS, adb, VA-API, PyAV, VB-Cable, VB-Audio, v4l2loopback, FFmpeg, USB, Wi-Fi, JPEG, MJPEG, H.264, fps, Nightly, file names, commands, {fields}, %1$s.

## Framing speeds, slowest first (keep short)
Schnecke, Träge, Gemächlich, Ruhig, Gelassen, Stetig, Flott, Schnell, Fix, Blitz

## Room to move (keep short)
Off, Fidget, Shift, Lean, Step, Roam -> Aus, Zappeln, Rutschen, Lehnen, Schritt, Herumgehen. Its tooltip: "zappeln, auf dem Stuhl rutschen, dich lehnen, einen Schritt machen oder im Raum herumgehen"
