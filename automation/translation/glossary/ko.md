# Korean glossary and style

The agreed terms for Korean. Use them in every text. If one seems wrong, keep it and raise an objection with the reason. Never change a term silently.

Avoid: 당신, 귀하, 너, 어플, 핸드폰, 셋팅, 유저, 다운로드 받다, 하드웨어 가속화

## Style
- Do not use second-person pronouns (never use "당신", "귀하", or "너"). Popular Korean apps (e.g. Toss, Apple, Slack) address the user naturally by omitting pronouns altogether.
- Register: Use polite, friendly, and natural honorific style (해요체: ~해요, ~하세요, ~해 보세요) for conversational text, guides, tooltips, and dialogs. Avoid stiff bureaucratic tone (~하십시오체 / ~합니다). No slang.
- Buttons use concise noun forms or nominalized verb forms ("휴대폰 추가", "다시 시도", "업데이트 및 다시 시작"). Statuses use concise progressive forms ("Wi-Fi로 스트리밍 중") or state nouns ("준비됨", "스트리밍 중 아님").
- Keep the English's brevity. A note saying "keep short" is a hard limit.
- Keep the English " - " (spaced hyphen). No en or em dashes. Use the same quotation marks as the English ("...", '...').
- In prose and numbers, use a decimal point (2.4 GHz). Do not put a space before % and °C ("{level}%", "%2$d%%", "36.5°C").
- Questions in dialogs: "~할까요?" (not "~하시겠습니까?")
- Card names in prose: "X 카드" (브라우저 카메라 카드).
- Pronouns and app references: Telescope on desktop is "Telescope" or "컴퓨터 앱". The phone app is "휴대폰 앱" or "앱".
- Keep untranslated: Telescope, OBS, adb, VA-API, PyAV, VB-Cable, VB-Audio, v4l2loopback, FFmpeg, USB, Wi-Fi, JPEG, MJPEG, H.264, fps, Nightly, file names, commands, {fields}, %1$s.

## Framing speeds, slowest first (keep short)
달팽이, 거북이, 느긋, 차분, 침착, 안정, 경쾌, 빠름, 날렵, 번개

## Room to move (keep short)
Off, Fidget, Shift, Lean, Step, Roam -> 끔, 꼼지락, 들썩, 기울임, 한 걸음, 돌아다님. Its tooltip: "꼼지락거리기, 의자에서 자세 바꾸기, 몸 기울이기, 한 걸음 걷기 또는 돌아다니기"
