Telescope

Use your phone as a webcam, with every lens and full manual control.

Free and open source. No watermark, no ads, no account.

Change the lens and settings from your computer

Switch between the ultra-wide, main and telephoto lens and set exposure, white balance and focus in the desktop app while it streams.

Up and running in about two minutes

  1. Get the desktop app

    On Windows run the installer (it installs only for your user and needs no admin). On Linux extract it and run ./start.sh.

  2. Follow the checklist

    It sets up the virtual camera, puts the phone app on your phone with a QR code and pairs the two.

  3. Pick it in your app

    Choose Telescope (Windows) or Phone Camera (Linux) as the camera, like any other webcam.

What Linux needs

Python 3.12 or newer and v4l2loopback for the virtual camera. USB also needs adb but Wi‑Fi works without it.

Debian and Ubuntu
sudo apt install v4l2loopback-dkms
Fedora and Nobara (Fedora needs RPM Fusion first)
sudo dnf install v4l2loopback

Manual setup covers other distros and Ubuntu 22.04's older Python.

Plugins for work and for fun

Most of the desktop app is plugins. Switch on the ones you want and leave the rest off, or install ones other people made.

  • Auto framing

    Zooms and pans to keep you in the shot, and frames two people together.

  • Background and Chroma key

    Blur the room or swap it out, with or without a green screen.

  • Desk mode

    Point the phone at a sheet of paper and calls see it flat and head-on.

  • Fun pack

    Clone party, an invisibility cloak, light painting, VHS, a 1998 webcam and lots more.

  • Colour grading

    Lift, gamma and gain wheels, with a grade saved for each phone.

  • Several cameras in one picture

    Your face big and the desk in a corner, for apps that only take one camera.

  • And more

    Privacy blur, a Name tag, Notes next to the lens, an On Air sign for the people you live with, QR codes, Cinema tools and full quality Photos.

Questions people ask

Is it actually free?

Yes. Every feature, every resolution, no watermark, no ads. If you'd like to chip in anyway, there's Ko-fi.

Will it work with Discord, Zoom, Teams, OBS or my browser?

If it can use a webcam, yes. Telescope shows up there as an ordinary camera and you pick it like any other one.

I have an iPhone or a Mac.

The phone app is Android only, but Browser camera streams from an iPhone's browser with nothing to install. The desktop app runs on Windows and Linux, not macOS.

Will it work on my phone?

It needs Android 8 or newer. Device compatibility lists what's been tested, and if yours isn't there let me know how it went.
