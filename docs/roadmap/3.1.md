# 3.1 (30 September to 2 October 2026)

Written afterwards from the commit history, the issues and the release notes for [3.1.0](https://github.com/LunarKittyy/Telescope/releases/tag/v3.1.0), [3.1.1](https://github.com/LunarKittyy/Telescope/releases/tag/v3.1.1), [3.1.2](https://github.com/LunarKittyy/Telescope/releases/tag/v3.1.2) and [3.1.3](https://github.com/LunarKittyy/Telescope/releases/tag/v3.1.3).

## What it was about

Making Light keep up with whatever connection it's on. 3.0 made it the default, and on weak Wi-Fi a fixed bitrate either wasted quality or lagged. 3.1.0 added Dynamic bitrate (as much as the link carries, backing off before the video lags), raised the slider to 100 Mbps and added the "Can't keep up" note with a button for what to try next ([#88](https://github.com/LunarKittyy/Telescope/issues/88)). FPS became a dropdown that greys out rates the camera can't do.

## The patch releases

- 3.1.1 fixed Automatic streaming never stopping a stream you started yourself ([#115](https://github.com/LunarKittyy/Telescope/issues/115)) and the "Can't keep up" note showing up while the camera was still starting. Temperature and Tint stopped snapping to set spots, since there's no one right white balance to snap to.
- 3.1.2 was about security. **Local only** really became USB only, the Windows camera driver moved into Program Files, the Linux config got locked to your user and pairing became harder to jam from the network.
- 3.1.3 faded out every card but Connection until a phone is paired, so a fresh install points at the one thing to do first.

## Left open

Dynamic only changed the bitrate. On a link too thin even for its lowest bitrate the frame rate stayed where it was and the frames got smeared.
