## Auto colour grading

A button on the Colour grading card for people who don't want to set the grade up themselves. It measures about a second of frames and fills in the existing sliders to give a starting point anyone can still tweak rather than a black box. A cheap laptop camera with a hand-made grade, auto framing and a light background blur already looks like a proper webcam. This gets most people there with one press.

It looks for people through the vision pack (and fetches the pack if it isn't there yet), then fits the basic sliders in stages. The card shows a short note on what it did, an Undo and a brighter and darker nudge. What it measures, all as numbers from the frames:

- White balance from the face rather than the full frame, since a coloured wall throws off the usual "make the average grey" approach. Skin of every tone sits close to one hue line and Temperature and Tint turn the face's average colour onto it. With no face it corrects a cast across the picture more gently
- Gamma so the face lands around middle brightness and Gain so the brightest part of the background doesn't clip. Dark scenes get lifted and overexposed ones brought down
- Lift and Contrast from the histogram, to put the darkest bit a little above black while a flat picture gets some depth
- Highlights from how much of the frame is close to clipping, like a window or a lamp behind you
- Saturation towards a target, held back by the skin so faces don't go orange

## Stats

An optional Stats card that shows what is going on in a stream as it happens, for anyone who wants to know why a picture stutters or likes numbers. OBS has one and people check it first when something drops, but it can only see what reaches it.

It has two views.

**Standard** has what people usually expect from a stats panel, enough to tell whether the phone, the link or the computer is the slow part:

- Frames per second in and out, repeated and dropped frames and the reason for each
- The delay from the phone's camera to the virtual camera
- Wi-Fi or USB, the Wi-Fi band and signal and the bitrate against what was asked for
- The phone's temperature and whether it's throttling
- Which apps read the virtual camera

**Nerd** adds more of what can sensibly be measured, following a frame from the phone to the virtual camera:

- On the phone: encoding time, the encoder's queue and what the phone was asked for against what it does
- On the link: round-trip time, jitter, lost and resent packets, the clock offset between phone and computer and each part's share of the delay
- On the computer: decoding time and where it runs, each frame step's time and whether it runs on the card or the CPU, when the balancer moved the steps and why
- What each plugin's steps cost, to find a slow one
- For the app itself: CPU and memory

Each number gets a small graph of the last minute that still shows a spike after it passed. Copy diagnostics adds a summary of the Nerd view. Every line has a star that pins its number to the bottom bar (the star shows on hover). The MCP server's `stats` and `watch` read the same numbers.

A plain-language line saying what's slowing the stream was tried on the card and in Copy diagnostics and taken out again.

## An MCP server

An optional plugin, off by default, that an AI app like Claude Desktop can use to read and change Telescope's settings. Someone can describe the look they want or ask what's making the picture lag. The AI app starts a small `telescope mcp` command that talks to the running Telescope over a local socket without opening a network port. [docs/mcp.md](../mcp.md) has the details.

What got built from the idea:

- `describe`, `get`, `set` (several settings at once, checked first and applied only if every value is right), `revert`, `measure`, `status`, `diagnostics`, `actions` and `run_action`, plus `stats` and `watch` for the Stats numbers. `measure` is the same measuring Auto colour grading uses
- Each AI app only undoes its own changes and a setting you moved yourself afterwards is left alone
- An MCP button in the top bar shows when an AI app is connected and undoes what it changed. The MCP tab has a Read only switch and the last 30 things the AI did
- A control the AI changes gets an outline for a moment, when it's on screen
- Plugins declare their settings (Colour grading and Transforms so far)
- Starting or stopping the stream, switching the phone's camera off and picking another phone are out of reach

Sending frames from the camera isn't built and neither is a preview where changes wait until accepted. They're in [Some day](future.md#more-for-the-mcp-server).

## Older DSLRs over USB

Cameras that only do live view over USB (many older DSLRs) show up in the device picker next to the webcams. It's Linux only. The Linux download includes libgphoto2 with nothing to install. When it's missing the app falls back to the `gphoto2` command. The picture is small (a Nikon D5100 sends 640×426 at about 30 fps). When the camera ends live view after a few minutes the stream shows the last frame and carries on.

It went further than the first plan:

- The Webcam card gets what the camera allows right now (a focus jog with a Focus button, aperture, exposure compensation and white balance)
- The Photos card takes photos with the DSLR, including RAW and JPEG together, and recovers after the stream drops
- A table of camera quirks, `rust/crates/outputs/src/webcam_capture/quirks.rs`, that stops one camera's habits from spreading through the code

## Fixes from a review of the port

A review of the Rust desktop in November 2026 looking at threads, error handling, `unsafe` code and the dependencies. Most of what it found only shows when something already went wrong and a normal stream looks the same. Shipped:

- The Windows 11 camera waits at most 30 ms for an app that's reading it and drops that frame rather than freezing
- A webcam writer that won't stop can't get a second one started next to it and the window no longer waits on it
- A JPEG decoder that crashes only loses that frame and three crashes in a row reconnect with fresh decoders rather than leaving a frozen picture
- Over UDP the first keyframe request goes out straight away and frames skipped while waiting for a keyframe count as arrived again like in the Python app
- `cargo deny` checks the dependencies' advisories and licences every week (`.github/workflows/deny.yml`)

## The new look

The desktop window got redone, with IBM Plex Sans as its font. Shipped:

- Design tokens for every size, radius, spacing and colour and a UI kit of controls built on them (`STYLE.md` is the guide). All that used to have its own hard-coded colour is on tokens now
- Four looks to pick in the Look section of General: Dark, Classic (the old deep blue), OLED and Light
- Every card rebuilt on the kit, with an icon rail, sliders whose fill shows what they mean, dials, wheels and a pad for Desk mode's sheet size
- A shared motion kit (switches, sliders, folds, pages, banners and menus all move the same way) that follows the system's reduced-motion setting
- Tooltips with a title, what the control does and a hint, quiet while someone is adjusting and quick while reading
- Settings window redone in the same style and built the first time it's opened rather than at start
- Framing got steadier. Levels end at the head, chest, belt and feet, zoom follows the Max zoom setting and the Framing bubble always shows a stand-in scene. Shape is called Aspect ratio now and Things in hand is Show hands
- The pan map outlines the phone's longer lenses and a zoom change on the phone is followed in the frames so the picture doesn't jump
- KDE Plasma title bars take the app's colours

## Lighter on the processor and memory

Work on running many effects at 1080p and 4K. Shipped:

- The Fun pack effects are much cheaper and Clones and cloak skip a frame with nothing taken
- Full-frame results go into reused buffers without copying the frame first. Zoom and stretch reuse their buffers between frames
- The preview's tint, zebras, peaking and false colour work off the window's thread and a folded Stats card does nothing
- The QR reader reuses one worker and reads the brightness plane
- Quarter turns work in blocks and Light decodes the next picture while the last goes through the frame steps
- Starting without opening Settings uses 122 MB, down from 173 MB, since Settings is built on its first open

## Hardware decoding on by default

On Linux it's on by default when the card is VA-API, including for a config that never chose. A choice made in Setup always wins. Windows and other cards are off until switched on. The card is asked for NV12 so a game's work on the card doesn't hold the copy up. The decoder in a child process is [dropped](dropped.md).

## A simpler Privacy blur

With more than one person in the picture it blurs all of it or shows the wait screen. **Blur the others** and **Blur all but me** are gone and count as **Blur the whole picture**, including on keys bound to them.

## Filters and lens correction

A Filters card for finishing the picture the way a photo editor would. It's optional and switched on under Look in the Plugins tab, like the other picture cards. Denoise, dehaze, skin smoothing, sharpen, clarity, vignette, glow and film grain sit on it. A filter at 0 is off and costs nothing. Denoise has an over-time option that averages the parts of the picture that hold still, which cleans up a dim room far better than blurring does. Skin smoothing uses the face model from the vision download and is greyed out until that's there.

The card points to the phone's own Sharpening and Noise reduction on the Camera card rather than repeating them, since using both looks worse than using one.

Transforms gets a Lens correction slider that straightens the curved lines a wide or long lens makes. Faces near the edge stop looking stretched. Edges are cropped or pulled in only as far as needed to fill the picture.

## High quality cutout and Cut-in

Background gets a **High quality cutout** switch that uses Robust Video Matting for cleaner edges around hair and hands. It's off by default. The model is its own optional download and runs through ONNX Runtime, which is loaded at runtime rather than linked. While the model is missing, still loading, has failed or is running behind, the normal MediaPipe mask is used and the picture doesn't wait for it. The Windows package ships the C++ runtime DLLs ONNX Runtime needs.

Cut-in sits next to Edges and pulls the cutout's border in. Less of the room shows around the outline. 0 is the old behaviour and it works with both masks.

## The download banner

Downloads of the vision pack and the high quality cutout show a banner with a progress bar. If one fails, the banner turns red and shows the error.

## 5.1.1 (14 November 2026)

A patch ([release notes](https://github.com/LunarKittyy/Telescope/releases/tag/v5.1.1)) for the vision pack download, plus small fixes and polish from the new look.

- When nothing is using the camera and the window is minimised or in the tray, the effects stop until an app opens the camera or you open the window. The stream from the phone remains up and resumes at once
- Room to move in Auto framing shows a bubble while you drag it, with a box for how far you can move before the framing follows
- The restart bar in Settings > Plugins has a Revert button that undoes the plugin switches you changed
- A phone that gets too hot lowers the frame rate first and then the picture size. When it gets critical it pauses the camera until it cools down and the stream and mic remain up
- The vision pack download no longer fails on slower internet, since it had a time limit on the entire download. The same went for adb, VB-Cable and update downloads
- Installing the vision pack zip through Install in Settings > Plugins works now and is no longer turned away as a too big plugin. Picking the zip while it's still downloading or installing shows a warning rather than doing nothing
- Telescope uses the graphics card your system picks, which is not always the fastest one. A laptop set to its built-in graphics is meant to run on it
- Browser camera and webcams are remembered as the last pick again rather than going back to the first phone
- The restart bar slides all the way out and no longer shrinks into a line
- Presets, Updates, Devices, Photos and Stream output have their icons back in Settings > Plugins
- Turning on Values in the colour picker no longer moves the switch away from your cursor
- The corners of the colour picker's square no longer let light through
- Chroma key's Edges slider handle takes the colour under it, like the others
- The gaps around the card columns are the same width on every side
- The window uses a little less processor with a lot of cards open
- Cut-in in Background has its own icon

## 5.1.2 (14 November 2026)

A patch ([release notes](https://github.com/LunarKittyy/Telescope/releases/tag/v5.1.2)) for Telescope closing right after the splash screen on some Windows laptops.

- On some Windows laptops with two graphics cards, 5.1.1 closed right after the splash screen. Telescope now picks the graphics card more carefully and moves on to the next one if a card doesn't work
- If Telescope can't start on Windows, a message box shows the reason and offers to open the log, rather than Telescope closing without a word
- Turning on Values in the colour picker near the top of the window no longer moves the switch away from your cursor either
- When a hot phone pauses the camera, the desktop notes that under the heat warning rather than showing a frozen picture

## 5.1.3 (16 November 2026)

A patch ([release notes](https://github.com/LunarKittyy/Telescope/releases/tag/v5.1.3)) that fixed Intel graphics on Windows ([#489](https://github.com/LunarKittyy/Telescope/issues/489)) and brought an early 3D mode and a Reset on every card.

- Intel cards on Windows run through DirectX 12 first, since the Intel Vulkan driver crashed while Telescope opened the card. A card that was skipped after a crash gets another try once its driver changes
- On laptops with two cards Telescope follows Windows' graphics setting for it (the faster card unless it's set to Power saving) and hardware decoding opens the same card as the rest of the picture work.
- 3D from two phones, as a Lab plugin. Side by side or red/cyan on the Telescope camera, paired by capture time, with Fine tune sliders and Match for exposure and colour. Lining up with a printed sheet, the WebXR page and the rest remain in [future.md](future.md#3d-from-two-phones)
- A Reset on every card and every section in Settings, plus Reset all settings
- Cards can be reordered from a card's header or Layout in Settings
- Plugins can carry a Lab tag (an early version) or a Limited tag (has limits that won't go away)
