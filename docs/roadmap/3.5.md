# 3.5 (16 October 2026)

Written afterwards from the commit history, the issues and the [release notes](https://github.com/LunarKittyy/Telescope/releases/tag/v3.5.0).

## What it was about

Letting people switch off the parts of the desktop app they never use and add plugins other people made. **Plugins…** lists every part that can go, says what goes missing before switching one off and applies changes after a restart. **Install plugin…** takes a shared `.zip`, starts it switched off and warns before it runs, since a plugin can do anything on your computer.

Most of the work wasn't visible. The desktop's parts used to reach into each other, and `app.py` had grown big from [3.3](3.3.md)'s several cameras at once ([#188](https://github.com/LunarKittyy/Telescope/issues/188)). 3.5 took that apart so every plugin but Connection only talks to the others through a small fixed set of calls and copes with being switched off. `desktop/ARCHITECTURE.md` writes the rules down, and `desktop/tests/test_architecture.py` and `desktop/tests/test_plugin_removal.py` check them.
