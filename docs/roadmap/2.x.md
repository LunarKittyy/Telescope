# 2.x (June to August 2026)

Written afterwards from the commit history and the [release notes](https://github.com/LunarKittyy/Telescope/releases).

## Before 2.0: PhoneCam

The repository starts on 15 June 2026 with a desktop app carried over from an earlier project called PhoneCam. It was one big script, and the phone's stream was open to anyone on the network. The first days went into zoom and pan, a device picker with settings per phone, bundling adb and UnityCapture, and the first CI builds.

## 2.0 and 2.1 (20 June)

2.0 rewrote that script as a `telescope` package with one plugin per card in the window. The project got renamed from PhoneCam to Telescope the same day, together with QR pairing, Local only (binding the phone's server to 127.0.0.1) and the first preview window.

2.1 followed a few hours later with a big batch of camera controls: white balance as continuous Kelvin plus tint instead of six presets, exposure compensation, noise reduction, sharpening, black level lock and the torch. White balance was marked as only partly working at the time.

## 2.2 (14 July)

A fullscreen preview on the phone for aiming it without looking at the computer, with lens pills and crop guides. On Linux, a fix for adb picking the wrong device when several were plugged in, and an option to keep the v4l2loopback config across reboots.

## 2.3 (9 August)

The window got rebuilt around three columns. The desktop could start and stop the phone's camera itself for the first time, with resolution and FPS taken from what the phone really supports. USB pairing went through adb, **Copy diagnostics** showed up, and every request to the phone started needing a token from pairing (before that anyone on the network could watch). The license changed from CC BY-NC 4.0 to AGPL-3.0, and plugins started going through a typed `HostServices` API instead of reaching into the window's internals.

## Left open

The token traveled over plain HTTP without encryption, every update meant downloading the new release by hand and there was no microphone yet. August after 2.3 was quiet apart from fixing the resolution picker ([#12](https://github.com/LunarKittyy/Telescope/pull/12)) and splitting it into aspect ratio and size.
