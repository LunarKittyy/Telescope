# 3.2 (4 October 2026)

Written afterwards from the commit history and the [release notes](https://github.com/LunarKittyy/Telescope/releases/tag/v3.2.0).

## What it was about

The microphone from 3.0 worked, but it recorded the way a phone call does (tuned for a phone right at your mouth), so from across a desk it came through very quiet. 3.2 switched the phone to record the way the camera app does for video. The Microphone card got the tools to see and fix the level: a meter that holds the latest peak, **Mute** (also in the tray menu), gain from -24 to +12 dB (further in Advanced) and a limiter that holds sudden loud bits under the top instead of letting them crackle.

## Left open

The mic only ran alongside the camera. The desktop dealt with the phone's and the sound card's clocks drifting apart in a basic way, by cutting the buffer back whenever it grew too long.
