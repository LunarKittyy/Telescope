# 4.4 (28 October 2026)

Planned in October 2026 after 4.3.1 ([release notes](https://github.com/LunarKittyy/Telescope/releases/tag/v4.4.0)). It started as a small batch and became 4.4 once webcam support turned out too big for a patch release. The webcam code got two code reviews the night before the release and the fixes went in with it. PyAV 19 still didn't make it (aiortc has no release that allows it yet) and moved on to the release after.

## Translations for the 4.3 plugins

The 4.3 batch added 545 texts that none of the six languages has yet. Until they do the new plugins show in English everywhere. The translation site didn't get them either. Once they're translated the site gets the new texts and a new build to show the new plugins in its previews.

## Webcams and capture cards

A webcam plugged into the computer shows up in the phone picker under its own name and streams like a phone would, with every effect and a virtual camera of its own. Cameras with a USB webcam mode and anything plugged into an HDMI capture card count as webcams too, which covers most of what people mean by DSLR support. Capture goes through OpenCV (V4L2 on Linux, Media Foundation on Windows) and Telescope's own virtual cameras never show up in the list.

The Webcam card offers the camera's own resolutions and frame rates and a controls panel built from whatever the camera reports (focus, exposure time, gain, white balance, anti-flicker and the like). On Windows any control that doesn't work reliably gets hidden.

A Logitech C920 and an HP 320 FHD were tried by hand on Linux, unplugging mid-stream included. Windows only ran in a VM with the HP passed through, since there's no Windows PC to test on. Merged in #381, with fixes from two code reviews in #386.

## UDP on Wi-Fi moves to Settings

The switch leaves the Connection card for the Streaming tab of the Settings window and stays off by default. On real Wi-Fi it stutters more often than TCP does and it shouldn't sit up front looking like a headline feature. Anyone who switched it on keeps it on. Merged in #382.

## Black screen on the phone

A button next to Preview in the phone app turns the screen black while the camera keeps going, like the one on Browser camera's page. Double tap wakes it. Some phones' power saving still kills the stream now and then even with battery optimisation off. An app that stays on screen should get killed less often (the screen being black saves battery on OLED phones too). Merged in #383, and #385 stops TalkBack from reaching the controls under it.

## 4.4.1 (28 October 2026)

A patch on the same day ([release notes](https://github.com/LunarKittyy/Telescope/releases/tag/v4.4.1)) with what came up when 4.4 got tried by hand with two phones and a webcam.

- A phone on a cable doesn't pair by itself anymore. **Add phone** lists every plugged-in phone by its model name with a **Pair** button each and the list updates as phones get plugged in or out. Pairing while another phone streams leaves the picker where it was instead of switching over and ending that stream. Merged in #396
- Black screen became a small moon icon left of Preview. The screen only dims once the status text has faded, since dimmed text was hard to read. Merged in #395
- Four streaming edge cases: a second USB unplug left the port forward stale, a quick Stop and Start could overlap, choosing USB for one phone could restart another phone's stream, and Windows looked webcams up by an index that shifts when cameras come and go (now by device path). Merged in #393
- The desktop log says how the app was closed (the window, the tray, a plugin or a restart), which helps tell a crash from someone quitting. Merged in #394
- The new texts got translated in #399

## 4.4.2 (28 October 2026)

Another patch on the same day ([release notes](https://github.com/LunarKittyy/Telescope/releases/tag/v4.4.2)) with ten new languages and what turned up while looking at them.

- Ten first-draft languages, marked (beta): German, French, Russian, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Turkish, Polish, Italian, Korean and Vietnamese. Merged in #401 to #409 and #412, with their rows in `docs/translating.md` in #410
- `text_fit.py` now pretends a phone is paired and picks every option on every card, which found texts cut off on the Curves page, in word readouts like Dynamic and in the flip buttons. Those got fixed in the same PR, #415
- MediaPipe sent Google a usage report whenever a model loaded, with no setting to turn it off. The vision pack build now blocks it and the release has new vision packs. Merged in #416
- The emulator pass waits for Install over USB to finish before it opens the phone app (#414), and a pull request that only bumps `VERSION` skips the three builds (#418)
