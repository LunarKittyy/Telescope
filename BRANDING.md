## What you can do

- Redistribute the official builds as they are, logo included.
- Write about Telescope in articles, reviews, videos or docs and use the name, logo and screenshots to refer to the real app.
- Say that your project is a fork of Telescope or based on it, in plain text.

## What you can't do

- Use the name or logo for a modified version or a project of your own, or in a way that suggests I made or approve of it.
- Copy the banner, the website design or the screenshots into your own project.

If you fork Telescope, give it its own name and replace the files above with your own. The code is yours to use under the AGPL-3.0.
